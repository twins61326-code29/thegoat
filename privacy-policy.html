Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

NovaCalc respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the NovaCalc calculator app handles information.

Information We Collect

NovaCalc does not collect, store, or share personal information. The app does not require an account or registration.

Calculation History

Calculation history is stored locally on your device using your browser's local storage. This information is not sent to us or shared with third parties.

Internet Access

NovaCalc does not require an internet connection to perform calculations.

Third-Party Services

NovaCalc does not use third-party services that collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

NovaCalc does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on Google Play.
